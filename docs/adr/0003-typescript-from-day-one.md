# 0003 — TypeScript from day one

## Context

For me, I want to know the error before I launch the app. So TypeScript is better than JavaScript.

## Decision

I chose TypeScript. First, it is a newer technology. Second, because it is better to find bugs with it, and to connect Laravel with it. I will not stay not knowing what is happening until I launch the app locally or in production — I will know it while coding. That is why I chose TypeScript.

## Consequences

More time spending because I am not good at JavaScript, but it is something new to learn, and I am ready to take that step.

## Status

Accepted, 2026-08-21.
